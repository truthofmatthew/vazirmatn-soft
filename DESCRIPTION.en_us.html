Vazirmatn Soft is a rounded version of Vazirmatn, the Persian and Arabic typeface by Saber Rastikerdar. Dots are round, stroke ends are round and corners are soft, while the letterforms, joins and spacing stay as in Vazirmatn. It covers Persian, Arabic and Urdu, with Latin from Roboto.

The family has nine weights from Thin to Black and a variable font. It adds compact signs for تومان and ریال (type ء after the word), lowered digits and other options as stylistic sets.

Vazirmatn Soft is dedicated to the memory of Saber Rastikerdar. To contribute, see github.com/truthofmatthew/vazirmatn-soft.
